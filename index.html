About NavAble

Navigation shaped around people.

NavAble is an Android accessibility mapping and navigation app created to make everyday travel easier to understand and plan. It combines useful place information, community contributions, AI-assisted photo analysis, and human verification in one experience.

The goal is simple: help more people know what to expect before they arrive.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about NavAble, its accessibility information, and the upcoming app.

Is the NavAble app available now?

The Android app is still being prepared for release. You can explore the features on this site now and check back for availability updates.

How is accessibility information verified?

NavAble combines community-submitted details and photos with automated checks and staff review so that useful information can be confirmed before publication.

Can I report information that is outdated?

Yes. Community updates are an important part of NavAble. The app is designed to let people report changed entrances, routes, facilities, and other access conditions.

What kinds of accessibility needs does NavAble support?

Place and route details can cover step-free access, ramps, elevators, curb cuts, parking, restrooms, surface conditions, and other mobility-related needs.
