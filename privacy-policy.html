Privacy Policy

This page outlines the privacy areas that should be finalized before the NavAble website and mobile application are released publicly.

Draft project notice: replace this content with a policy reviewed for your actual Firebase, Google Maps, Roboflow, analytics, and mobile-app configuration before deployment.

Information NavAble may process

Depending on the final implementation, NavAble may process account details, accessibility preferences, saved locations, map searches, contributed photographs, reports, reviews, and approximate or precise location data.

Why information is used

Information should only be used to provide accessible navigation, maintain user accounts, analyze submitted accessibility features, moderate community data, improve accuracy, and protect the service.

Third-party services

The final policy should explain how Firebase, Google Maps, and Roboflow process information and link to their applicable privacy documentation.

Administrative access

Authorized staff access should be logged and limited through authentication, role-based authorization, and database security rules.

User choices

Users should be able to review applicable permissions and request access, correction, or deletion of personal information according to the policies and laws that apply to the deployed service.
