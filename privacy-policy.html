NavAble app and website

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy covers the NavAble mobile app and this website. It describes what NavAble actually collects, who can see it, and how to ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data.

What NavAble collects

Your name, email address and password (handled by Supabase Authentication; NavAble never sees your password); your profile photo, if you add one; the reports, photos, pinned locations and AI scan results you submit; bug reports you send, including basic device information; and the points you earn.

What other users can see

Your full name, profile photo, level and points appear on the Top Contributors leaderboard. Places and photos that admins approve are shown to everyone. Your email address, reports in review and bug reports are visible only to you and NavAble admins.

Your location

With your permission, your location is used on your phone to show the map, find nearby places and plan routes. It is sent to the place search and routing services below as the search or route starting point, and is stored by NavAble only when you pin a location in a report.

On your phone only

Saved places, your theme and text size choices, and which level-up celebrations you have seen are stored only on your device.

Services NavAble uses

Supabase stores accounts, reports and photos. Google Maps Platform powers Explore search, and Directions opens Google Maps. OpenStreetMap services provide the map, place search and routes. Photo scanning runs on your phone when possible; otherwise the photo is sent to Roboflow for analysis.

This website

The website does not use accounts, cookies or analytics for visitors. When you send a bug report through the contact form, your name, email address, the bug details and any device information you type are saved to NavAble's database so the team can follow up. Other messages from the contact form open your own email app and are sent from your email account. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts; these services receive your IP address when the page loads.

Administrative access

Only NavAble staff with an active admin account can review reports, photos, bug reports and user accounts. Access is checked by sign-in and by the database's security rules.

Your choices

You can edit your name and photo, or remove your photo, in your profile. To ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data, including your reports, send a request through Report a bug in the app's Reports tab, or email nav4ble@gmail.com.

Data Privacy Act

NavAble handles personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
